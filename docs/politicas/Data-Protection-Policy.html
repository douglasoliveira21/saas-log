1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for protecting personal data in the company: why and how data is processed, who is responsible, how the rights of data subjects are honoured and how the company demonstrates compliance with the LGPD.

2. Roles of the company

  • Controller: for the data of the users of the platform (account, sign-in, security and support data).
  • Processor: for the data that customer companies enter in the platform, such as records of their own customers, suppliers, orders, deliveries and tax documents. The customer company is the controller of that data and is responsible for its legal basis and for informing its data subjects. The company processes it only to provide the service and under the customer's instructions.

3. Principles

All processing follows the principles of the LGPD: purpose, adequacy, necessity (minimization), free access, data quality, transparency, security, prevention, non-discrimination and accountability. In practice, the company only collects the data needed for the service, uses it only for the stated purpose, keeps it only as long as necessary and protects it as described in the security documents.

5. Rights of data subjects

  • Data subjects may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about sharing, and revocation of consent, by writing to suporte@vgon.com.br.
  • Requests are registered and answered within the legal deadlines (a complete declaration within 15 days for access requests). The identity of the requester is verified before data is disclosed.
  • When the data was entered by a customer company, the request is forwarded to that company, which is the controller, or the requester is told how to contact it. The company assists its customers in answering such requests, including by providing, correcting, exporting or deleting the data.
  • Each request is kept in a log with the date, type, decision and response date.

6. Sharing, providers and international transfers

  • Personal data is shared only with providers needed to operate the service (hosting, e-mail and productivity services, notification services, and the sales channels and tax services the customer chooses to connect), with public authorities when required by law, and with legal or accounting advisers under confidentiality.
  • Providers are engaged under data processing and confidentiality terms and must provide an adequate level of protection.
  • The platform is hosted on a server in the United States. This international transfer is carried out with the safeguards provided in the LGPD, and the Privacy Policy informs data subjects about it.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept while the customer account is active and for the period required to meet legal obligations and to defend rights (for example access records and the audit trail).
  • Driver location points are deleted automatically after 30 days by default.
  • When a seller revokes a sales channel authorization, the stored credentials for that channel are discarded.
  • When a customer contract ends, the customer's data is made available for export for a reasonable period and then deleted or anonymized, except where the law requires it to be kept. Backups are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

8. Privacy by design and impact assessment

New features, integrations and changes that process personal data are assessed for privacy before release: what data is needed, who can see it, how long it is kept and how it is protected. For processing that may create high risk to data subjects (for example continuous location tracking), the company documents the assessment, limits collection to what is necessary (for example, only while a route is in progress) and informs the data subjects.

9. Security and incidents

Personal data is protected by the measures described in the Information Security Policy and related standards: role-based access, isolation between customer companies, encryption, network segregation, logging and backups. Suspected personal data breaches are reported immediately and handled under the Incident Response Policy, including notification of customers, data subjects and the ANPD when required.

10. Records, training and governance

  • The company keeps a record of its processing activities (this policy's section 4 and the related inventory), updated when processing changes.
  • The DPO (suporte@vgon.com.br) is the point of contact for data subjects and for the ANPD and advises on compliance.
  • All personnel receive data protection guidance when they join and at least once a year, and are bound by confidentiality.
  • Compliance with this policy is reviewed at least annually, and the results are reported to management.
