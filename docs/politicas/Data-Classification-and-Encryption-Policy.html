1. Purpose

This policy classifies the information handled by the company and defines the protections, including encryption in transit and at rest, that each class requires. It supports the Information Security Policy and the Data Protection Policy.

2. Data classification

ClassExamplesRequired protection
RestrictedPasswords; API keys, OAuth tokens and app secrets for sales channels; digital certificates; two-factor secrets; encryption keysNever stored in clear text. Encrypted or hashed at rest; encrypted in transit; never written to logs, e-mail, chat or source code; access only by the service that needs them.
ConfidentialPersonal data of users and end customers (names, tax IDs, contacts, addresses); orders, prices, stock, financial and fiscal data; audit trail; backupsEncrypted in transit; access by role and need to know; isolated per customer company; backups encrypted; not used in test environments.
InternalOperating procedures, architecture documents, non-sensitive configurationShared inside the company and with providers under confidentiality.
PublicMarketing material, privacy policy, terms of useNo restriction.

Information is classified by its owner when it is created or collected. When in doubt, the higher class applies. Customer data remains the property of the customer company, which is the controller of the personal data of its own customers; the company acts as processor.

3. Encryption in transit

  • All communication between users' browsers, the mobile application and the platform, and between the platform and third-party services (marketplaces, tax authorities, e-mail), uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Cookies that carry sessions are marked secure and cannot be read by scripts.
  • Notifications received from sales channels (webhooks) must carry a secret token and are accepted only after the sender's cryptographic signature is verified.
  • Product photos are delivered only through signed, expiring URLs.
  • The database has no public port; it communicates only with the platform's own services through the server's internal network.

4. Encryption and protection at rest

DataProtection
User passwordsNever stored; only salted hashes created with the scrypt key-derivation function.
Credentials and tokens for sales channels, digital certificates, two-factor secretsEncrypted before being stored, with AES-256-GCM. The key of each customer company is derived from a master key (HKDF, salted with the customer company identifier), and each encrypted value is bound to its company and purpose, so a value from one customer cannot be decrypted or swapped into another. The master key supports rotation (current and previous key).
Database backupsEncrypted with AES-256-GCM before being stored, using a dedicated backup key that must be kept separately from the backup files. Restoration is tested periodically.
Business and personal data in the databaseProtected by access control and role-based permissions, row-level isolation between customer companies, network segregation (no public database port, firewall limited to the service ports) and the provider's infrastructure controls. Column-level encryption of this data is not applied; protection of the underlying storage is evaluated at least once a year in the review of this policy.
Company workstationsDisks are encrypted (Windows BitLocker), as set out in the Endpoint Security Standard.

5. Key and secret management

  • Encryption keys and application secrets are kept in the server environment configuration, never in source code or repositories, and are accessible only to those who operate the platform.
  • Keys are rotated when a person with access leaves or when exposure is suspected. Rotation keeps the previous key available only for the time needed to re-encrypt existing data.
  • Loss or suspected exposure of a key is handled as a security incident under the Incident Response Policy.
  • Secrets are masked before being written to logs or shown in any interface.

6. Handling, retention and disposal

  • Restricted and confidential data is accessed only by authorized people, for legitimate purposes, according to the Access Control Policy.
  • Production data is not copied to test or development environments, and customer data is not copied to removable media or personal devices.
  • Data is kept only as long as needed for the service or required by law. At the end of a customer contract, data is made available for export and then deleted or anonymized, except where retention is required by law. Backups are retained for a limited period and then deleted.
  • When a seller revokes the authorization of a sales channel, the stored credentials for that channel are discarded.

7. Responsibilities, exceptions and review

The Information Security Officer owns this policy and verifies compliance at least annually. All personnel must handle information according to its class. Exceptions must be requested in writing, approved by management and have an end date. Violations may lead to disciplinary action or contract termination.
