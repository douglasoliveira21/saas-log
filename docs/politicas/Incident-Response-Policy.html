1. Purpose

This policy defines how the company detects, reports, handles and learns from security incidents, who is responsible for each step, and how customers, sellers, sales channels and authorities are informed.

2. What is an incident

A security incident is any confirmed or suspected event such as: unauthorized access to systems or data; exposure or loss of customer data, credentials, tokens or keys; malware or ransomware; account takeover; loss or theft of a device with company access; abuse of an integration with a sales channel; denial of service; unauthorized changes to the platform; or a significant failure of a security control.

3. Roles and responsibilities

RoleResponsibility
Everyone (employees, contractors)Report any suspected incident immediately to suporte@vgon.com.br; do not try to investigate or hide it; preserve evidence (do not delete messages, logs or files).
Information Security Officer (incident lead)Receives reports, classifies severity, leads the response, decides containment actions, keeps the incident record, coordinates communication and runs the post-incident review.
EngineeringInvestigates technical causes, contains and eradicates the threat, restores services and data, and implements corrective measures.
Data Protection Officer (DPO)Assesses the impact on personal data, advises on notification duties under Brazil's LGPD, and is the contact for data subjects and the ANPD (suporte@vgon.com.br).
ManagementApproves critical decisions (for example, taking the service offline, notifying authorities) and provides resources.

4. Severity levels

LevelDescriptionTarget response
CriticalConfirmed exposure or loss of personal or customer data, compromise of keys or credentials, ransomware, or a platform-wide outageStart immediately; incident lead engaged within 1 hour
HighCompromise of an account or integration, or suspected data exposure under investigationStart within 4 hours
MediumContained event with limited impact (for example, blocked attack, isolated malware on one device)Within 1 business day
LowPolicy violation or weakness with no evidence of impactWithin 5 business days

5. Response process

  1. Detect and report: incidents are detected by internal monitoring, the audit trail and logs, alerts from providers, or reports from staff, customers or researchers. Every report is logged with date, time, reporter and description.
  2. Assess and classify: the incident lead confirms whether it is an incident, assigns a severity level and opens an incident record.
  3. Contain: stop the spread without destroying evidence. Typical actions include disabling affected accounts, revoking sessions, disconnecting affected integrations and invalidating their tokens, rotating exposed keys and passwords, blocking addresses at the firewall, and isolating affected systems.
  4. Eradicate: remove the cause (malware, vulnerable component, unauthorized access) and close the weakness.
  5. Recover: restore services and, if necessary, data from verified backups; confirm normal operation and keep monitoring closely afterwards.
  6. Review: within 5 business days after closure, the team documents the timeline, root cause, impact, what worked and what did not, and corrective actions with owners and dates.

Evidence is preserved for the investigation: audit trail, application and server logs, and affected files. The platform's audit trail and integration logs record sensitive actions with user, time and customer company, with secrets masked.

6. Communication and notification

WhoWhen and how
InternalThe incident lead informs management at once for Critical and High incidents, and keeps the team updated throughout.
Affected customers (customer companies and sellers)Without undue delay once an incident that may affect their data is confirmed, by e-mail to their registered contact, describing what happened, the data involved, the measures taken and what they should do.
Sales channels (for example TikTok Shop)If data obtained through a channel, or the credentials of the integration, may be affected, the channel and the affected sellers are notified without undue delay and within the period required by the channel's terms. The integration is disconnected and its credentials revoked or rotated.
Authority (ANPD) and data subjectsWhen the incident may cause relevant risk or damage to personal data subjects, the company, together with the controller, notifies the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and the data subjects within the legal deadline set by ANPD regulations (currently three business days from becoming aware of the incident), with the information the regulation requires.
Other partiesPolice, insurers, providers and legal advisers are contacted when appropriate, by decision of management.

Public statements are made only by management. Communications are factual, avoid speculation and never include secrets or personal data.

7. Incident record

Each incident has a record kept by the incident lead, with: identifier, dates and times, reporter, description, severity, systems and data affected, actions taken, notifications sent, root cause, corrective actions and closure date. Records are retained for at least five years and are available for audits and to partners on request.

8. Preparedness and testing

  • Backups are taken at least daily, are encrypted, and restoration is tested at least twice a year.
  • Contact details for the response team, hosting provider and key suppliers are kept up to date.
  • This policy is rehearsed at least once a year through a simulated incident (tabletop exercise), and the results are used to improve it.
  • Personnel receive guidance on how to recognize and report incidents when they join and at least once a year.

9. Exceptions and review

Exceptions must be approved in writing by management. This policy is reviewed at least annually and after every Critical incident.
